Reading question

Through what instruments does the order operate, and where do the gaps between design and the value chain appear?

What it looks at

Manifestation follows the concrete forms the order takes: licensing, designation, marking, access restriction, enforcement. It looks for the point where those mechanisms stop producing the visibility or control their design assumed, where the instrument on paper and the instrument in the field come apart.

In the cycle, Manifestation sits where the line meets the field and bends: the Friction stage.
