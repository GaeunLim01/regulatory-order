Reading question

How is the boundary of this order drawn, and where is it tested or redrawn?

What it looks at

Ordering follows how a regulatory order is assembled: how its jurisdictional or use boundary gets drawn, who claims the authority to set it, and where technological or distributional change puts that boundary under pressure. It reaches past formal rulemaking to the advisories, designations, and quieter moves through which a line actually gets fixed.

In the cycle, Ordering sits where the line is first drawn and later redrawn: the Focus and Re-response stages.
