Ordering

How regulatory order is configured and changes over time.

The frame

Trace the rules, authorities, institutional arrangements, and boundary changes through which a regulatory order takes shape and evolves. This is not limited to enactment or rulemaking.

02

Boundary change

03

Authority & institutional arrangement

Who has authority to define, interpret, implement, or alter the order?

04

Temporal trajectory

How has the order changed, accumulated, narrowed, expanded, or been revised?
