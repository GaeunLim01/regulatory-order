THE SHAPE THEY SHARE

Four stages, run in a different order each time.

Read in sequence, the five cases move through the same four stages. Where each one enters, and where it slows, is what differs. The heavier a line runs, the longer the case sat in that stage.

The four stages

Trigger

A technology surfaces, or comes to be treated as a matter of national security. The moment a rule now has something new to account for.

Boundary

Regulation draws a line to catch it, inside existing rules where it can, through a new article where it cannot.

Friction

The line meets the field and bends: circumvention, conflict over what it covers, resistance from the people it lands on.

Re-response

Something follows the friction: the existing rule gets amended, or a new regulation is enacted to catch what it missed.

Reading each path

01 Super MicroEnters where the line already exists, then sits in friction as the diversion runs, before a late re-response in the indictments. 02 Remote Access Security ActYears redrawing the boundary again and again inside old categories, until a new article is written. 03 Anthropic and the Dept. of WarReaches friction as a contest over what the model may be used for, and stays there, unresolved in court. 04 Fable / MythosAll four stages compressed into about three weeks, from restriction to staged restoration. 05 Claude watermarkingStarts at the boundary stage with the legislation itself, then holds a long friction as the mark is edited away downstream.
