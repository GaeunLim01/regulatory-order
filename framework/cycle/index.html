THE SHAPE THEY SHARE

Four stages, run in a different order each time.

Read in sequence, the five cases move through the same four stages. Where each one enters, and where it slows, is what differs. The heavier a line runs, the longer the case sat in that stage.

The four stages

Trigger

A technology surfaces, or comes to be treated as a matter of national security. The moment a rule now has something new to account for.

Focus

The law draws a line to catch it, inside existing rules where it can, through a new article where it cannot.

Friction

The line meets the field and bends: circumvention, conflict over what it covers, resistance from the people it lands on.

Re-response

Something follows the friction, a redrawn line that restarts the sequence, or an end left open.

Reading each path

01 Super MicroEnters where the line already exists, then sits in friction as the diversion runs, before a late re-response in the indictments. 02 Remote Access Security ActYears of focus, the boundary redrawn again and again inside old categories, until a new article is written. 03 Anthropic and the Dept. of WarReaches friction as a contest over what the model may be used for, and stays there, unresolved in court. 04 Fable / MythosAll four stages compressed into about three weeks, from restriction to staged restoration. 05 Claude watermarkingStarts at focus with the legislation itself, then holds a long friction as the mark is edited away downstream.

This cycle is the when: the order events run in. Look inside any one stage, case by case, and it splits into who draws the line, through what, and onto whom.
