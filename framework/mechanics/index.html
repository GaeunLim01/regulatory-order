THE ACTORS AROUND IT

Three mechanics, read at any point in the cycle.

Regardless of where a case sits in its cycle, three mechanics read how the actors around a regulatory order (regulators, providers, courts, users) interact with it as a shared object. Something is bounded before it can be operated, operated before its weight can be distributed.

Ordering → Manifestation → Distribution
MECHANISM 01

Ordering

Who draws the line, and what they fix it on.

Reading question

How is the boundary of this order drawn, and where is it tested or redrawn?

What it looks at

Ordering follows how a regulatory order is assembled: how its jurisdictional or use boundary gets drawn, who claims the authority to set it, and where technological or distributional change puts that boundary under pressure. It reaches past formal rulemaking to the advisories, designations, and quieter moves through which a line actually gets fixed.

A boundary can stay contested across Boundary and Friction alike, whenever the parties never agreed on what was even in question in the first place.

MECHANISM 02

Manifestation

Through what instrument the order operates.

Reading question

Through what instruments does the order operate, and where do the gaps between design and the value chain appear?

What it looks at

Manifestation follows the concrete forms the order takes: licensing, designation, marking, access restriction, enforcement. It looks for the point where those mechanisms stop producing the visibility or control their design assumed, where the instrument on paper and the instrument in the field come apart.

It shows up most often during Friction, when an instrument built for one setting meets a field that can't run it as designed. But the same strain can appear earlier, at the moment the instrument is chosen, before it's ever tested against the field.

MECHANISM 03

Distribution

How the weight of the order moves, and onto whom.

Reading question

How do the informational demands and the burden of this order move across the actors it reaches?

What it looks at

Distribution follows how the order travels outward: who issues, receives, interprets, or carries it; where formal duty lands versus where the operational knowledge actually sits; and how cost, discretion, and accountability get redistributed as it moves down the chain. Information asymmetry and where responsibility settles are the recurring things it surfaces.

Burden can land once, during Friction, or keep moving outward past it, from the first actor it reaches to whoever handles the object next.
