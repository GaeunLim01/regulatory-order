Reading question

How do the informational demands and the burden of this order move across the actors it reaches?

What it looks at

Distribution follows how the order travels outward: who issues, receives, interprets, or carries it; where formal duty lands versus where the operational knowledge actually sits; and how cost, discretion, and accountability get redistributed as it moves down the chain. Information asymmetry and where responsibility settles are the recurring things it surfaces.

This mechanism isn't fixed to one stage either. In Fable / Mythos the burden landed on the provider during Friction. In the watermarking case it kept moving outward past Friction too, from provider to user to whatever tool the content passed through next.
