Analytical lens · 03

Distribution

How norms, information, documents, authority, and regulatory burden move across actors.

The frame

Trace how a regulatory order reaches different actors, through which channels, and with what allocation of authority, information, obligations, or burden. Information asymmetry and responsibility allocation are central observations under this lens.

Open question A prompt for reading the case, not a required field.

How do informational requirements and responsibility burdens created by this order move across actors?

What this lens typically surfaces

Who issues, receives, interprets, or bears the order; the documentary and institutional channels through which it travels; where formal requirements land versus where operational knowledge concentrates; and how compliance costs, discretion, and accountability are redistributed. In cycle terms, Distribution is most closely tied to transmission into the value chain and to the asymmetric allocation of burdens that follows breakdowns.
