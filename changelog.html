Changelog

What changed, and why

Structural revisions are recorded here because the reasoning behind them is part of understanding the framework, not administrative overhead.

v3 · Current

Three independent modules

Removed the single tree

v2 nested everything under one branching tree with Event Type at the top. This produced recurring friction: items belonging to one line of inquiry kept appearing as awkward children of the other. Modules 1 and 2 answer independent questions and are now separate axes, tagged simultaneously.

Made Event Type's two-step branch explicit

The earlier three-column layout of Enactment, Amendment, and Conflict obscured that Enactment exits at Step 1. Its blank cells were reading as missing data. The branch is now drawn as a branch.

Separated Module 3 in kind, not in rank

Module 3 does not classify cases. It describes a downstream process that applies once an order exists, whichever module produced it. It sits beneath both axes rather than beside them.

Added quantitative fields, to Module 3 only

Nine numeric fields on Transmission, Distribution, and Re-formation. Modules 1 and 2 stay purely qualitative — scoring a classification judgment manufactures false precision.
