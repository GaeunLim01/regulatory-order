AI GOVERNANCE × REGULATORY ORDER

Reading regulation: before the outcome, after the design

When a new technology falls under export control, I check the design record before I read the outcome: whether the rule had caught up to this yet, or whether it had already accounted for it.

Compliance asks whether a line was crossed. This asks whether the line existed yet, and if it did, who was supposed to be watching it.

What that reading answers

Object held

The rule has to pick a thing. A chip. Remote access. A nationality. A file.

Where it started to move

Sometimes it sat outside the categories on the books. Sometimes the categories already named it, in writing, and it moved anyway.

Where cost arrives first

A subsidiary, a shipping manifest, a stock price, before the parent company or the regulator finishes deciding who is liable.

Difference carried

A gap someone else designed, held by the firm anyway. Sometimes by an employee instead.

This order isn't arbitrary. Whoever wrote the categories and whoever only interprets them decides where a gap can form, and a gap nobody designed for asks a different question than a warning the rule had already put in writing. Checking which one a case is comes before checking how it ended.
