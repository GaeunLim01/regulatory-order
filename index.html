AI GOVERNANCE × REGULATORY ORDER

Reading regulation: before the outcome, after the design

When a new technology falls under export control, I check the design record before I read the outcome: whether the rule had caught up to this yet, or whether it had already accounted for it.

Reading method

Layer What How
Object held The regulation has to pick a thing. A chip. Remote access. A nationality. A file. Checked first, because nothing else in the row can be read until this is fixed.
Where it started to move Sometimes it sat outside the categories on the books. Sometimes the categories already named it, in writing, and it moved anyway. Checked against the date the categories were written, not the date the case was decided. A missing category and an ignored warning look like the same event from outside; they aren't from here.
Where cost arrives first A subsidiary, a shipping manifest, a stock price, before the parent company or the regulator finishes deciding who is liable. Traced actor by actor: who acted into the gap, and whether what they meant to do lines up with where the cost actually landed.
Difference carried A gap someone else designed, held by the firm anyway. Sometimes by an employee instead. Followed past the actor who caused it, to whoever is left holding it. Usually not the same person.
