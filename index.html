AI GOVERNANCE × REGULATORY ORDER

Reading regulatory order across AI governance.

How regulatory order is formed, how it reaches actors through information and documents, and how responsibility and burden are distributed. Selected cases are examined through three parallel lenses; a recurring cycle of boundary-setting and reconfiguration runs across them.

Regulatory order cycle

Full pattern →

A recurring dynamic: technological change creates category mismatch; authorities set or clarify a boundary; the boundary is transmitted into the value chain; application reveals gaps; limits become visible; reconfiguration may follow.
