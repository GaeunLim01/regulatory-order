Framework v1.0

Reading regulatory order

One procedure, five layers, tracing the pattern a regulatory order leaves in the documentary record. A set of worked examples read through it.

Why this

Regulatory systems work by taking hold of a thing: naming it, placing it, timing it, and assigning someone to answer for it. Where AI is concerned, that grip keeps slipping, and it slips well before enforcement is ever reached. Rules get drafted against an object that has already moved, and what arrives at the actors standing under them is not what the drafters described.

This project reads the marks that leaves in the documentary record. It is less interested in whether a given rule was right than in what happened to the object it was written for: how it was defined, by whom, against what, and what reached the firm standing at the other end. The five layers are that reading procedure, set out separately from the papers it came from so that it can be applied to a case other than the ones it was built on.

Methodology

How does regulatory order form, and how does it reach the actors standing under it?
  • A Classification: why is the object unstable
  • B Formation: how the boundary reached its current shape
  • C Manifestation: how it shows up in the record
  • D Distribution: who ends up holding it
  • E Re-formation: what happens next
