Module 1 reads conditions; Module 2 reads events. A condition holds whether or not anyone came into opposition. An event requires that something happened. Every case carries values on both — a case can be an Enactment and still show a full set of instability conditions.

Scope

Regulatory orders that national or international authorities currently form or operate through documented rules, formal classifications, and administratively recognizable decisions. Tracing is confined to the documentary record: published rules, official gazettes, consultation records, court filings, industry comments. Exchanges that never entered that record are out of reach — this does not mean such paths don't exist, only that this method cannot see them.
