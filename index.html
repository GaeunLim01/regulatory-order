02 · Regulatory Order

The analytical problem

Rather than asking only what a rule says, the framework traces how governance becomes legible, attributable, documentable, and actionable for the actors standing under it.

Formation · Transmission · Trace · Distribution · Re-formation

Documentary orientation

Governance is read through its traces.

The method follows published rules, official decisions, consultation records, court filings, industry comments, contracts, and related records to reconstruct how an order is formed and how it reaches actors. It treats the documentary record as the place where unstable objects, competing definitions, timing gaps, and distributed burdens become observable.
