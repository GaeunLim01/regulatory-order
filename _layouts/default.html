Scope. This framework applies to regulatory orders that national or international authorities currently form or operate through documented rules, formal classifications, and administratively recognizable decisions. Tracing is confined to the documentary record. Exchanges that never entered that record are out of reach; this does not mean such paths don't exist, only that this method cannot see them.
