4c Measured fields

Defined here rather than scattered through Module 3. Leave blank if undocumented.

Numeric: a field earns a number only if two fixed points in the record determine it
FieldUnitTwo points
Deadline gapdaysFormal effective date ↔ actual preparation or compliance gate
Time to responsedaysOrder taking effect ↔ first documented firm response
Enforcement amplification eventscountThe original order ↔ subsequent investigations, penalties, comparable-case citations

A field that needs a judgment about where to stop counting ends up measuring how much was reported rather than what happened. The scope limit here is documentary, so that judgment is unavoidable once a field scales with coverage.

Narrative: recorded in prose, not comparable across cases
FieldWhat to record
Financial exposureproseCost, fine, or revenue loss attributable to the order. State whether reported, estimated, or modeled.
Downstream reachproseParties the burden propagated to. Name them where identifiable; where the bearer is diffuse, describe its character instead of counting.
Fields dropped from v3, and why
Documentation burdenNo counting rule. A single form with five schedules is 1 or 5 depending on who fills it in.
Concurrent order loadValue depends entirely on how wide the analyst draws "bearing on the same actor".
Time to normalizationBroke on first application. The Anthropic case already required staged recording rather than one number.
Contextual amplification eventsCounts co-occurrence; widen the window and the number grows.

Modules 1 and 2 carry no numeric fields and none may be added. Scoring a classification judgment manufactures precision rather than adding information.
