Current framework

Five analytical layers

The framework classifies and records the same case across five parallel layers. The layers are not a sequence and do not imply that one produces another.

A

Classification

What kind of thing is being treated as the object of the case?

Object typeCapability · Access & data · Physical goods · Relational
Instability conditionsJurisdictional overlap · Speed mismatch · Object opacity · Category-practice mismatch
B

Formation

How is the instance classified in terms of how its boundary took shape?

Event typeEnactment · Dispute · Amendment
Formation traceSummary, dated markers, and the point at which the order began operating when the record supports it.
C

Manifestation

Which manifestations are recorded for the case?

Representative vocabularyBoundary drift · Definitional contest · Competing clocks · Recurring reset · Classification decision
Additional vocabularyDeadlines · Contractual relations · Documentation systems · Supply-chain position · Access restriction
D

Distribution

Which positions and mechanisms are recorded for where consequences sit?

PositionsSource · Bearer
Bearer typeIdentifiable, nameable · Unbounded population of individuals; cannot be enumerated
LeverageLow · Contested · High
MechanismsBurden displacement · Downstream propagation · Enforcement amplification
E

Re-formation

Which re-formation responses are recorded for the case?

Re-formationSupply-chain restructuring · Product redesign · Contract rewriting · Jurisdictional relocation · Lobbying · New intermediaries
Contextual amplificationExternal shocks that raise the stakes attached to an order: war, sanctions realignment, currency shock, political turnover, or financial crisis.
Framework rule

The five layers are recorded side by side. A value in one layer must not be used to infer, suppress, or overwrite a value in another layer.

The controlled vocabulary remains provisional. A case that does not fit is recorded as a deviation rather than forced into an existing value.
